# Kyuva

Kyuva is a free, local-first teleprompter for Mac. It keeps a compact always-on-top script near the camera for video calls, recordings, lessons, product demos, presentations, and prepared talks.

## Input to result

Write, paste, or import a plain-text script. Place the overlay beside the camera, then pace it by fixed speed, words per minute, or a target finish time.

## Public release

- Price: free
- Availability: all 27 European Union storefronts

- Publisher: Mykyta Dudnichenko
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/ie/app/kyuva/id6804827338?mt=12

## Privacy boundary

Kyuva stores scripts and settings locally. The public release has no account, subscription, advertising, analytics, tracking, or required cloud service.

## Important limits

- Kyuva is a normal macOS window and may appear in a screen share or recording. Check the preview before presenting.
- Mac App Store version 1.0 uses timed pacing. Voice Follow is development-only until a future reviewed update.
- iPhone and Apple Watch versions are in development and are not publicly available.
