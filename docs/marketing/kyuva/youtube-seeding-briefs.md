# Kyuva tutorial seeding briefs

These are walkthrough briefs, not ad scripts. Do not contact creators or publish
on their behalf without a fresh approval.

## 1. Make a 60-second product update without memorizing it

- Audience: indie developers and product builders
- Show: fill the free template, paste it into Kyuva, set a finish time, record
  one real feature update, then show the finished clip
- Proof required: actual Kyuva window, real pacing, capture disclosure
- CTA: template first; App Store second

## 2. Keep project-update notes beside the webcam

- Audience: remote workers and team leads
- Show: turn five status bullets into short lines, place Kyuva near the camera,
  pause and resume with the keyboard
- Proof required: real call preview; no invisibility claim
- CTA: download the free Mac app

## 3. Kyuva to captioned demo through KikuAI

- Audience: Nick's product audience and solo creators
- Show: draft a truthful script, rehearse in Kyuva, record another KikuAI
  project, then optionally process the video through Automatic Subtitles
- Boundary: Automatic Subtitles is a separate server-processed paid workflow;
  state upload and retention terms on screen
- CTA: the demonstrated project first, with Kyuva linked as the workflow tool

## Asset checklist

- 16:9 real desktop recording;
- clean Kyuva overlay close-up;
- finished output clip;
- App Store icon and one real screenshot;
- capture visibility disclosure;
- one campaign link matched to the publishing channel.
