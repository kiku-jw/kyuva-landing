# Kyuva visibility brief

Status: active, bounded free Mac release

## Entity brief

- Product: Kyuva
- Publisher: Mykyta Dudnichenko
- Category: free local-first teleprompter for macOS
- First user: someone reading a script near the camera during a call, product
  demo, lesson, recording, presentation, or prepared talk
- Job: keep words close to the lens and pace them without an account or cloud
- Public proof: Mac App Store listing, public source, five real screenshots,
  support/privacy pages, 53 core tests, signed universal build evidence
- Differentiator: simple App Store installation, local scripts, flexible timed
  pacing, no account, ads, analytics, subscription, or required cloud
- Hard limit: the overlay is a normal macOS window and may appear in captures
- Unreleased: Voice Follow update, iPhone app, and Apple Watch companion

## Visibility wedge

Lead with one narrow promise: **a free local-first Mac teleprompter for scripts
beside the camera**. Do not compete on hidden-from-capture claims until exact
application-specific proof exists.

The owned proof loop is:

1. draft one short product-demo script;
2. rehearse it in Kyuva;
3. record a real walkthrough for another Nick project;
4. optionally caption it with the separately disclosed KikuAI Automatic
   Subtitles workflow;
5. publish the useful walkthrough with one attributable App Store link.
